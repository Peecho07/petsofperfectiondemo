Privacy & SMS policy

Plain-English answers about what we collect, why, and how texting works.

The short version: we use your info only to care for your pet and run your appointments. We never sell it, and we never share your phone number or text-message consent with anyone for their marketing.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us when you contact us, book an appointment or opt in to text messages:

  • Contact information: name, phone number, email address and physical address.
  • Pet information: your pet's name, breed, size, grooming history and any care notes you share.

2. How we use it

We use your information only to provide grooming and bathing services, manage your appointments and contact you about them, such as reminders and a note when your pet is ready for pickup.

3. How we share it

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We use a small number of service providers, like our scheduling software and our text-message delivery provider, who process information only on our behalf and only to deliver our services to you.

4. Text messages (SMS)

By opting in to SMS on our website or in person, you agree to receive text messages from Pets of Perfection about your appointments and conversations with us. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out at any time.

Your mobile number and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Our text-message delivery provider receives your number only to send the messages you asked for.

5. Your choices

You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP. To update or delete your information, email info@petsofperfection.com or call us.
